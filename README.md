# KisanLink -- Agricultural Commerce & Direct Market Linkage Platform

Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Marketplace, Agri-Inputs (Fertilizers, Pesticides, Bio-Inputs, Tools), Real-Time Price Discovery, and Escrow Platform.

## Modules
- 1. Agri-Inputs Marketplace (Fertilizers, Pesticides, Bio-Inputs, Equipment)
- 2. Mandi Radar & Real-Time Price Discovery
- 3. Statistical Price Forecasting (AI Time-Series)
- 4. Multi-Factor Recommendation & Negotiation Engine
- 5. Digital Escrow & UPI Milestone Tracking
- 6. Field Dispatch Gateway (SMS & WhatsApp Simulation)
- 7. Agro-Climatic Weather & Spoilage Advisory
