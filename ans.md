### Purchase Cost & Transporter Fair Value

When proceeding with a purchase, the system should calculate two separate values:

1. **Total/Landed Cost**
   The total amount payable for the purchase, including the item’s quoted price, transportation cost, and any other applicable charges. This value should be displayed to both the **seller and buyer**.

2. **Fair Transportation Value/Fee**
   A system-calculated fair or maximum transportation fee based on factors such as the item value, quantity, distance, vehicle requirements, and other applicable transportation costs. This value should be displayed to the **seller, buyer, and respective transporter** and can be used as a reference for confirming or negotiating the transportation fee.

The **Total/Landed Cost** and **Fair Transportation Value** should remain separate so that the actual purchase cost is clearly distinguished from the recommended transportation fee.
